# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing disagreement on ideas and evidence; and
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing private information,
or other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project in another public space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may
warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants according to the
context, severity, and pattern of behavior.

For conduct involving GitHub content, use the content's **Report** action or
[GitHub's private abuse-reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
You may also flag the specific issue, pull request, or comment for maintainer
moderation without repeating sensitive details. Do not open a new public issue
containing a private conduct report.

Reports will be handled as privately as the platform permits. Retaliation
against someone who raises a good-faith concern is itself a violation.
